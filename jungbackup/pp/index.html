Jung Backup Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy covers Jung Backup, the open source backup app for GNOME, and this page about it. In short: Jung Backup does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It has no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no cookies.

Data the app handles

Jung Backup runs entirely on your own computer. It copies the files you choose to the storage location you choose (a local or network folder, an SFTP server, Google Drive or another rclone remote). Your files, settings, backup password and access tokens stay on your computer, in your system keyring, or at that storage location. None of it is sent to us or to any server we run, and we have no way to access it.

Google user data

If you choose Google Drive as the storage location, you sign in with Google in your own browser and the app requests only the drive.file scope. With it, the app can only see and change the files and folders it created itself; it cannot read anything else in your Drive. The app uses this access solely to store, list, restore and delete your backup files. The access token is stored in your system keyring on your computer. Google user data is never transferred to us or to any third party, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI or machine learning models. You can revoke access at any time in your Google account settings.

Jung Backup's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third-party services you choose

Storage providers you connect (for example Google Drive, an SFTP host or another rclone remote) process your backup data under their own privacy policies. The backups are encrypted by restic with your password before they leave your computer.

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts; as with any web request, these providers receive your IP address to deliver the page. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your rights

Since we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have questions about this policy, contact thomas@jungblut.io.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
