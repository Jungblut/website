How Ax1om Works

Perception, reasoning, and control in one learned stack

Multimodal Perception

Onboard vision encoders grounded in internet-scale pretraining—so robots recognize scenes, objects, and intent the way a foundation VLM does, not a brittle detector pipeline.

Language as the Interface

Specify skills in plain language—“pick up the desert item,” “clear the table”—and Ax1om translates semantic goals into motor plans without per-task fine-tuning.

Dual-Timescale Control

Slow semantic reasoning for long-horizon plans; fast reactive control for dexterous, closed-loop motion—the System-1 / System-2 pattern behind today’s frontier VLAs.
